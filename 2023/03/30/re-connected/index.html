SnatchTvGMarch 30, 2023

Hard work is necessary, and I am saddened that important fights have taken a back seat to current necessities. In my opinion, there is nothing more important than fighting against the negative forces in this world. If we can’t talk about these issues, how can we ever hope to solve them? And as for humor, it can be a powerful weapon of love. Someone who has a positive outlook can find humor in situations without being affected by negative emotions. We all have inner demons, and by sharing them with the world, we can confront and overcome them.”
