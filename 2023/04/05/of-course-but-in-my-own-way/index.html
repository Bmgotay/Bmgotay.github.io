Of course but in my own way..

SnatchTvGApril 5, 2023
Daily writing prompt
How have you adapted to the changes brought on by the Covid-19 pandemic?

Although it may not have been as many changes as people would think mostly it boils down to adjusting to the low level of education that has been unveiled. I was born at a time when natural immunity wasn’t a myth. When we all talked about cavemen but understood although we lived in a different time it did not necessarily mean we had changed very much. We grow stronger after stress our body gets stronger as we fight as does our mind. I am still unsure of my ability to speak even within my own platform but I am aware of the recent information about health risks of males between the ages of 15 and 25 being correlated to reactions brought on by recently made “treatments”. Private conversations allow more of an elaboration than anything in public. Hopefully, when America isn’t so dangerous for conservatives, the most sensitive things can be brought to the surface and handled as needed. Instead of this atmosphere of fear and coercion. #FauciOuchy
