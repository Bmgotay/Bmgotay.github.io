Rule the world

SnatchTvGApril 11, 2017

This music video features a creative blend of “Everybody Wants to Rule the World” by Tears for Fears and footage from the movie “The Bourne Identity.” The video showcases various intense and action-packed scenes from the film, seamlessly synchronized with the rhythm and lyrics of the song. The visuals are designed to create an immersive experience for viewers, conveying the thrilling energy and tension of the film. The music video is a dynamic and engaging tribute to “The Bourne Identity,” bringing new life to the classic song and providing fans of the movie with a fresh perspective on their favorite scenes.
