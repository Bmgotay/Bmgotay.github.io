Take Me To Church

SnatchTvGApril 11, 2017

This music video features scenes from the movie “Equilibrium” and the song “Take Me to Church” by Hozier. The movie scenes have been edited together to match the mood and lyrics of the song, creating a cohesive and visually stunning narrative. The video showcases the film’s themes of rebellion, freedom, and the fight against oppression, which are also echoed in the song’s lyrics. Overall, the combination of the movie and the song creates a powerful and emotionally charged music video that captures the essence of both the film and the music.
